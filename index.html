LinuxThinkpad Rclone

LinuxThinkpad Rclone is a personal integration used to access and back up files in the owner's Google Drive account using Rclone.

Privacy Policy

This application is intended solely for personal use by its owner.

Google Drive access is used to transfer, retrieve and manage the owner's own files and backups. The application does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics or third-party sharing.

Authentication credentials are stored locally. Backup contents are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive.

The application does not sell or share user data with third parties.
